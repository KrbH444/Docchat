# Northwind Trading Co. — Employee Handbook (sample)

This is a fictional handbook used to demonstrate DocChat.

## Working hours

Standard working hours are Sunday to Thursday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM, including a 30-minute lunch break. During Ramadan, working hours are reduced to 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Employees may request flexible hours with their manager's written approval, provided they are online between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM.

## Remote work

Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to two days per week. Remote days must be logged in the HR portal by 9:00 AM on the day. Remote work is not permitted during the first and last week of each financial quarter, when all finance and sales staff must be in the office.

## Expenses

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days with an itemized receipt. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to SAR 150 per day. Taxi and ride-hailing costs are reimbursed in full for client visits. Any single expense above SAR 2,000 requires pre-approval from a department director.

## Equipment

Each employee receives a laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every three years. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours. Employees may keep their laptop after leaving the company if it is more than three years old, after IT wipes it.

## Training budget

Each employee has an annual training budget of SAR 5,000 for courses, certifications, and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year. Requests must be submitted at least two weeks before the course start date.
